The AI Advantage

How generative AI transforms the customer journey — from 45-minute discovery calls to 5-minute self-serve design sessions that qualify leads automatically.

💬
Natural Language Room Design
Prompt → Configuration
Customer types: "Modern Bangalore apartment, master bedroom, warm colours, marble, no white walls." AI maps this to wall finish, floor, palette, furniture — and renders it in the 3D room instantly. Zero clicks on colour swatches.
GPT-4 / Gemini
🖼
Reference Image → Room Design
Web Image → Palette Extract
Customer pastes a Pinterest URL or uploads a photo they love. The system extracts the 5 dominant colours and 3 material types, matches them to Antara's finish library, and applies them to the 3D room — your actual materials, inspired by their reference.
Colour Extraction API

Photorealistic AI Render
Design → Real Image
The designed room becomes a photorealistic image — not a CSS box. Customers see their actual home, with their chosen finishes, rendered as a professional photograph. Delivered in 15–20 seconds. Included in every consultation PDF.

AI Style Quiz
5 Questions → Full Room

Phase 2 Deep Dive — Web Reference Import

Design from Anywhere

Customers don't think in finish codes — they save images. This feature bridges their inspiration to your actual material library in one step.

01
Paste Any Image URL
Customer finds a room they love on Instagram, Pinterest, or Architectural Digest. They paste the image URL into the visualizer. No screenshot, no upload needed.
PinterestInstagramHouzzAny URL
02
Colour Palette Extraction
The platform analyses the image and extracts 5 dominant colours using a Canvas-based colour quantisation algorithm — no server, runs in the browser in under 1 second.
Browser-nativeCanvas API0 server cost
03
Auto-Match to Antara Finishes
Each extracted colour is matched to the nearest finish in the Antara library using Delta-E colour distance. "Warm cream wall in reference" → Warm Ivory plaster. "Dark brown floor" → Dark Walnut wood.
Delta-E matchingAntara finish library
04
Apply to 3D Room Instantly
One click applies the matched palette to the live 3D room — walls, floor, ceiling updated simultaneously. Customer immediately sees their reference translated into a real Antara design.
Real-time 3D updateOne click
05
Side-by-Side Comparison
The reference image and the 3D room sit side by side. Customer can fine-tune — "make the floor warmer" — and the designer knows exactly what they're aiming for before the site visit.
Split viewFine-tune
06
Save to Project Board
All reference images and their matched palettes save to a project board. Customer arrives at the consultation with a fully defined brief — reference, palette, room, budget — reducing discovery time from 45 minutes to 5.
Project boardPDF exportWhatsApp share
